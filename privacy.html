Privacy Policy

VisualCrimes48 Upload. Effective November 4, 2026.

What this tool is

VisualCrimes48 Upload is a private tool that publishes videos to the VisualCrimes48 YouTube channel. Only the channel's operator signs in to it. It does not collect data from viewers or from anyone else.

Google user data the tool accesses

The operator signs in once with the channel's Google account. The tool asks Google for two permissions:

  • Upload videos (youtube.upload): to upload the channel's own videos, with title, description, publication time and thumbnail.
  • View the YouTube account (youtube.readonly): to read the channel's name, which confirms that the right channel is connected.

How the data is used and stored

  • The access credentials stay in a protected file on the operator's own computer. They are not sent anywhere except to Google when the tool renews its access.
  • The channel's name is only shown to the operator when connecting, and it is not stored.
  • The links of uploaded videos are kept on the operator's computer and sent as status messages to the operator's own Telegram chat. The link of a long video also appears in the description of the matching Short on YouTube.

Sharing

Google user data is not sold, and it is not shared with anyone apart from the status messages described above. It is not used for advertising, and it is not used to develop or train AI models.

The tool's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Deleting the data and revoking access

Access can be withdrawn at any time under Google account permissions. Deleting the credentials file on the operator's computer removes the stored access.

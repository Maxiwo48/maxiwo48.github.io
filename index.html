VisualCrimes48

Animated reconstructions of true crime cases, solved and unsolved.

The channel

VisualCrimes48 is a YouTube channel. Each episode tells one real case as a motion graphics film with narration.

VisualCrimes48 Upload

VisualCrimes48 Upload is a private tool that publishes the channel's finished videos. It uploads each video to the channel's own YouTube account, sets its title, description, thumbnail and publication time, and confirms which channel it is connected to. Once a week it reads how the channel's own videos performed and sends a private report to the operator.

Only the channel's operator uses this tool. It is not offered to anyone else, and it does not access other people's accounts.
